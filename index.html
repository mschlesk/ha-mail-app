Home Assistant Mail and Packages

This is a private, single-user app used by its owner to connect their own Home Assistant installation to their own Gmail mailbox.

It lets the open-source Mail and Packages integration read shipping and delivery notification emails over IMAP so Home Assistant can show package counts and delivery status for the household.

The app is not offered to the public and has no other users.
