Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

"Home Assistant Mail and Packages" is a private app used only by its owner to connect their own Home Assistant installation to their own Gmail account.

What data is accessed

With the owner's permission, the app signs in to the owner's Gmail mailbox over IMAP and reads shipping and delivery notification emails (for example from USPS, UPS, FedEx, Amazon and DHL) to count packages and show delivery status.

How data is used and stored

All processing happens locally on the owner's Home Assistant server at home. Email content is used only to produce package and delivery information for the owner's household. Delivery images (such as USPS Informed Delivery scans) may be stored on that local server.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party. Nothing is sent anywhere other than the owner's own Home Assistant server.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account's security settings (Third-party apps & services) or by removing the integration from Home Assistant.
